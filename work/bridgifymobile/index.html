Timeline

"How might we design a legal app that helps users identify and address common legal issues they might overlook in their daily lives?"

Overview

Simplifying legal challenges

Simplify legal challenges with features like case exploration, tracking, and an AI legal assistant. Bridgify offers a user-friendly interface, plain language guides, and quick access to resources — empowering users to manage legal matters confidently.

Context

Only 5–10% of people have a legal app on their phone

Despite the widespread use of productivity and social media apps, legal apps remain relatively rare. Only 5–10% of people have downloaded and used one — highlighting a significant gap in legal tech adoption.

Research

When asking people why they don't have a legal app, they often respond with "Why would I need it?"

1

People often don't realize the legal issues they face in everyday situations — leases, purchases, job agreements — leading to missed chances for protection and resolution.

2

People often avoid addressing legal issues due to fear of complexity and difficulty understanding legal language and procedures.

Opportunities

1

Provide users with resources and information through the app that clearly outline common legal issues they might face.

2

Use plain language and user-friendly tools to guide users through legal procedures, reducing fear of complexity and improving confidence.

Feature 1

Explore Legal Cases

Helps users find and evaluate available legal cases, identifying those most likely relevant to their situation.

Feature 2

Link Accounts

Link your external accounts and our AI will help identify the most relevant legal cases for you.

Feature 3

Track Your Cases

Track your case to see its current status, receive updates and news, and get guidance on your next steps.

Feature 4

Legal AI Assistant

Answers general legal questions, analyzes documents, and recommends relevant legal cases — all in plain language.
